About the studio

Live systems, not concept demos.

Multifunnels is an AI product studio for agentic search, commerce, and automation. Everything below is running in production today.

Multiple live consumer products AgentRoam, MoodTrip, Adin Flights, and GiftRoam serve real users across hotels, flights, connectivity, and global gifting.
MCP-ready platforms Four MCP endpoints expose hotel search, flight search, travel connectivity, and global gifting to AI agents.

Live inventory integrations Products search live hotel, flight, and eSIM inventory — not cached demo data.

Production monitoring and handoffs Analytics, error handling, and clear human handoffs keep every launched product measurable.
